## Hi there, I'm Núria! 👋
I'm a PhD researcher in **Biomedical NLP** at the University of Edinburgh, part of the [UKRI CDT in AI for Biomedical Innovation](https://ai4bi-cdt.ed.ac.uk/people/cdt-doctoral-researchers/2024-cohort).

My research focuses on making biomedical data easier to **find, understand and reuse**. I develop NLP and LLM-based methods to reconstruct experimental context from public datasets and the scientific literature, with a current focus on identifying transcriptomics experiments that are biologically compatible for joint analysis.

My broader interests include **scientific text mining, biomedical metadata, ontologies and knowledge representation, transcriptomics, reproducibility, and LLM-based tools for biological research**.
